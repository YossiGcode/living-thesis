The thesis

Three lines, one variant view, one way to be wrong

$189.5M

Space Systems already carries the company. Q2 2026 Space Systems revenue was $189.5M, up 94% year over year, and 81% of total revenue of $234.1M. Backlog is $2,355.9M: Space Systems $1,415.8M and Launch $940.2M, with about 45% due within 12 months.

−55%

Schedule news no longer moves the stock much. RKLB sits 55% below its $151.00 intraday high of May 27, 2026, yet four Neutron updates moved it only −3.7% to +1.4% over two days. Neutron targets about 13,000 kg to low Earth orbit in its reusable configuration.

$495M

Iridium adds a recurring network business. FY2025 revenue of $871.7M and OEBITDA of $495.3M. The deal is pending, with closing expected in mid 2027. Iridium's $1.775B Term Loan, carried as secured debt, stays outstanding and Rocket Lab USA, Inc. guarantees it at closing.

Variant view

The filings show a pattern: a second half 2025 target that moved to Q4 2026, then a launch window that is narrowing. The market already treats Neutron as late; recent schedule updates barely moved the stock. We treat the date as timing risk on a thesis that stands on Space Systems and, after closing, Iridium. The date decides when launch scales. It does not decide whether the company works.

What proves us wrong

A filing that discloses a thesis break: the Iridium merger terminated, Rocket Lab stating Neutron development is cancelled or suspended, or total backlog in a 10-Q more than 10% below the higher of the prior quarter and the trailing four quarter average. That is register entry R-003, the primary falsifier.

3 · What the filings say about Neutron's schedule

One target moved, one under pressure
FilingFiledNeutron timing language

The FY2024 10-K set a second half 2025 target. None of the three 2025 10-Qs repeated a date. In Q3 2025 the Key Factors paragraph on Neutron dropped "consistent with our targeted timeline" for "to a successful initial launch" and added "further delays". The FY2025 10-K reset the target to Q4 2026 after the January tank rupture. The Q2 2026 10-Q restated the milestone as pad delivery in Q4 2026, a softer target than launch, and said the window for an end of year launch is narrowing. No new launch date has been filed.

What it changes

The risk moves from cash runway to leverage and integration. Iridium's term loans stay outstanding after closing, with a downstream guarantee from Rocket Lab USA, Inc. and a higher rate.

There is no termination right tied to the RKLB price. Three stockholder suits over proxy disclosures were filed against Iridium and its board in New York (IRDM 8-K Sep 18, 2026). Their status after the vote is not yet confirmed.
